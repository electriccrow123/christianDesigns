U.S.S. STARHOME

Your room is the bridge.

Put on your headset. Starhome scans your room, cuts windows into your walls and turns your home into a starship. Fly through procedurally generated star systems, fight off pirates and boarders, and crew your ship with AI bots.

Checking for a VR headset...
  • Room scan maps walls, floor, couch and tables
  • Windows on any wall show open space
  • Bridge console by your couch: throttle, joystick, switches, keyboard
  • Armory: blasters, plasma saber, grenades, riot shield
  • Ship systems: phasers, torpedoes, shields, cloak, warp drive
  • AI crew: cleaning, chef, repair, security, medic and combat drones

Best on Meta Quest 3 / 3S / Pro with Space Setup done. Needs HTTPS. Progress saves in this browser.

Typing on the bridge terminal — Enter to run, Esc to stop

Desktop controls

Click look around (mouse) · WASD walk · C sit / stand

Left click press buttons, flip switches, fire held weapon · drag the throttle and joystick

Right click / G grab or drop the item you aim at · E use

Arrows pitch/yaw · Z/X roll · R/F throttle · Shift pulse boost

Space phasers · M torpedo · N next target

Tab tablet (shop, map, crew, build) · B build mode · T type on terminal · H this help
