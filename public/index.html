        Glossary

        How this game works

        • Prices are real market data (delayed up to ~15 minutes, depending on the exchange). Refreshes every 15 seconds.
        • Stocks fill at the last price. Options fill realistically: buys at the ask, sells at the bid (last price if there’s no quote).
        • Option commissions are $0.65 per contract (adjustable on Reset). Stock trades are commission-free.
        • Selling calls requires 100 shares per contract (covered calls). Selling puts reserves strike × 100 in cash (cash-secured puts). Short selling stock is disabled.
        • Limit and stop orders are “good ’til canceled” and are checked on each price refresh while the game is open.
        • Options left open at expiration are cash-settled at intrinsic value (in-the-money) or expire worthless.
        • Your game is saved in this browser. Use Reset any time to start over.
